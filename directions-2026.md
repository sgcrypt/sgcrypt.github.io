The 2026 workshop was held at [COM1, 02-12](https://nusmods.com/venues/COM1-0212), National University of Singapore.

To get to COM1, take the shuttle to COM3 (level B2), then use the elevator or stairs connecting to COM1. The talks were held on level 2 of COM1.

[Directions to COM3](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/about/getting-here/):

- Take the MRT to Kent Ridge MRT Station (CC24).
- Use Exit A, then follow signs to the bus stop opposite Kent Ridge MRT (bus stop 4).
- Take the NUS internal shuttle bus D2.
- Alight at COM3 (the third stop).

The [NUS internal shuttle bus](https://uci.nus.edu.sg/campus-life/campus-services/transportation/internal-shuttle-bus/) is free and does not require an NUS ID. These are historical 2026 directions; transport arrangements may change.
